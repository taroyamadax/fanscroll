# Privacy Policy — FanScroll

**Last updated: 2026-09-12**

## Summary

Drag Scroll does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data.

## Data collected

None. The extension does not collect any information about you or your browsing activity.

## Data stored locally

The extension stores a single boolean value (`dragScrollEnabled`) in Chrome's local storage to remember your ON/OFF preference. This value never leaves your device.

## Third-party services

None. The extension makes no network requests.

## Contact

If you have questions, please open an issue on the [GitHub repository](https://github.com/taroyamadax/fanscroll).
